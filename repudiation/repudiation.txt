- Briefly explain the vulnerability and why the secure version is better at preventing repudiation than the insecure version.
Ans: The vulnerability in the insecure.ts file is the absence of logging and accountability making it impossible to trace
who performed an action and when it occurred. This lack of accountability allows users to deny their actions without any accountability.
- What additional security mechanisms can you incorporate to prevent such attacks?
Ans: The additional security mechanisms we can incorporate are:
- Digital signatures to ensure users cannot deny their actions
- Immutable logs to prevent tampering or deletion
- Rate limiting and IP Whitelisting to  prevent users from trying to 
write the entire log space in memory
-  Ensure error messages do not expose sensitive information that could aid attackers
